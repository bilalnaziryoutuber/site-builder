Smart Tax-Saving Tips

Standard vs. Actual Auto Deductions

If you drive for business, choose standard mileage rates ($0.67/mile in 2024) or track individual costs like gas, lease, and repairs.

Qualified Business Income (QBI)

You may deduct up to 20% of your net business income off your federal income tax base. This is modeled in our progressive estimates!
